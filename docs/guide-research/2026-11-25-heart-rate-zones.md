# How do runners find their Apple Watch heart rate zones?

On November 25, US English [Google autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=how%20to%20see%20heart%20rate%20zones%20on%20apple%20watch) completed the same question with “during workout” and “after workout”. This establishes suggested wording. Search counts and the number of potential site visitors are unknown. A US Google Trends comparison with running cadence and treadmill distance returned HTTP 429.

In a [beginner 5K runner’s discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/AppleWatchFitness/comments/1shsra6/beginner_5_months_daily_5km_runner/), a commenter asked how another runner could see zones, and readers described two different screens. In a [second discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/AppleWatch/comments/13kxnl0/not_seeing_heart_rate_zone_information_on_my/), the author saw zones after a walk and during runs but could not find them during a walk. The guide answers both timing questions and explains that the enabled views depend on workout type.

Checked the published guides and open guide PRs for duplication. Apple’s Workout and Fitness steps come from the two Apple Watch User Guide pages linked in the article. Product source checks are recorded in public-release-facts.md. No physical-device test or demand-volume estimate.
