## Shipping-source verification

Read the versioned sources at app commit `6b27f42cf2a2febf33b9672906dce46a6794ba04` / `v1.3.5-b57-submission`. Exact paths and behavior are mapped in [public-release-facts.md](../public-release-facts.md#november-11-draft-import-and-follow-a-gpx-route).

Key result: the send confirmation is optimistic and the transport can queue delivery. The guide therefore tells runners to verify ROUTE READY or the saved route on the Watch, not to leave merely because the phone says Sent. Importing/sending is distinct from recording. The shipping navigation is Watch → Routes, not the unreleased You-tab design.

The GPX importer requires track points. A valid GPX made only of route points is not necessarily supported by 1.3.5. Map cache on iPhone does not establish offline Watch map availability. No offline-basemap, automatic-rerouting or exact-turn-coverage promise is made.

No personal records, real running routes, health data or live account activity accessed. No new screenshot is needed for these short steps; none was invented. An optional screenshot must come from the shipping app with a synthetic route after device access returns.
